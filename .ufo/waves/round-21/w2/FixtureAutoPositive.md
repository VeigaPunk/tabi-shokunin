Thus the synthetic positive is a structural counter control, not executable auto-positive lifecycle evidence. Missing scoped red control: coherently remove auto-positive's Completed response and submits, update receipt summaries and hashes, and require semantic outcome rejection if claiming the counter enforces that outcome. Existing `selftest-c1.mjs:31,53` fixture-edit/autosubmit-toggle mutants target the first case, not this auto-positive outcome. No mutant executed and no observed gate outcome claimed.
