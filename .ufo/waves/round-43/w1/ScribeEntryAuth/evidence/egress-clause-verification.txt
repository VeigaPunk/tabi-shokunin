## 3. The clause's FAILING state is plantable with no browser run

I planted a non-zero count into ONE sample of a hand-crafted receipt and re-ran the
frozen counter (direct invocation; no wrapper, no driver, no browser):

    findings: [run1-sample1-ss-egress, run1-sample1-proc-egress,
               run1-summary-ss-mismatch, run1-summary-proc-mismatch,
               run1-summary-egress-mismatch]
    egressDetail: [{run: run1, sample: 1, surface: ss,  remotes: [203.0.113.7:443]},
                    {run: run1, sample: 1, surface: proc, remotes: [203.0.113.7:443]}]

(b) CONSISTENT lie (same plant, all aggregates recomputed):
    findings: [run1-sample1-ss-egress, run1-sample1-proc-egress]

=> (a) shows the summary clauses DO fire on a partial lie; (b) shows they do NOT fire
   on a consistent lie. And in both, the egress clause itself emits its named findings
   and populates egressDetail — with no browser run anywhere. So the clause is testable
   offline in BOTH directions, which is what makes the two browser-free controls the
   peer proposes buildable.

## 5. Reproduction scripts (saved alongside this file)

                                      from a base receipt.json (browser-free)

                                      (zero counts + recompute every aggregate)

Both were re-run from a FRESH scratch directory against the frozen counter and reproduced
the findings above exactly. One reading caveat worth recording: a filter on the substring
"egress" hides run1-summary-ss-mismatch and run1-summary-proc-mismatch (those strings do
not contain "egress"), which makes a partial lie look like it fires only the egress
summary clause. The FULL findings lists are:

                             run1-summary-ss-mismatch, run1-summary-proc-mismatch,

                             receipt-binding-mismatch, witness-binding-mismatch]

                             witness-unreadable, receipt-binding-mismatch,
                             witness-binding-mismatch]

So: the partial lie fires three summary clauses; the consistent lie fires none; the
fully zeroed receipt fires none either. That is the whole of F8.
