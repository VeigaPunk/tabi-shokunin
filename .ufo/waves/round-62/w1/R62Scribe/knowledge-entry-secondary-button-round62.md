## What S165 (MDN) documents — the button mapping

The "Determining button states" section defines state via the `button` and `buttons` properties of `MouseEvent`, which `PointerEvent` inherits (staged raw 1981–1983), and tabulates device button states to values. The located row (raw 2014–2016, 6 whitespace-separated words plus two value cells):

> Right Mouse, Pen barrel button → `button` = `2`, `buttons` = `2`

The table's source note (raw ~2037–2039): "`button` indicates a change in the state of the button" — i.e. `button` is the *changed* button, `buttons` is the *held* mask. Admissible reading: S165 grounds the *values* a page observes for right-button press/release (changed `button` 2; held mask `2` while held, `0` after release). It supports interpreting page-side values; it does not make any browser run exist or behave.

## What S165 does NOT establish

The MDN table says nothing about: `contextmenu` dispatch timing (press vs release), event ordering relative to `pointerdown`/`mousedown`, cancellation semantics, `isTrusted`, `pointerId` stability, or cross-browser uniformity. Treating MDN as proof of any of those is the rejected overclaim (Gate below).
