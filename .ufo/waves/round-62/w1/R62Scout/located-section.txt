1980:     <!--lit-part V+arqU6ddcI=--><!--lit-node 0--><h2 id="determining_button_states" class="heading"><!--lit-node 1--><a class="heading-anchor" href="#determining_button_states">Determining button states</a></h2><!--/lit-part-->
1981:     <p>Some pointer devices (such as mouse and pen) support multiple buttons, and the button presses can be <em>chorded</em> (i.e., pressing an additional button while another button on the pointer device is already pressed).</p>
1982: <p>To determine the state of button presses, pointer events uses the <a href="/en-US/docs/Web/API/MouseEvent/button" title="button"><code>button</code></a> and <a href="/en-US/docs/Web/API/MouseEvent/buttons" title="buttons"><code>buttons</code></a> properties of the <a href="/en-US/docs/Web/API/MouseEvent"><code>MouseEvent</code></a> interface (that <a href="/en-US/docs/Web/API/PointerEvent"><code>PointerEvent</code></a> inherits from).</p>
