VERDICT: RED
------------
The criterion CAN fail and DOES fail on the r58 claim. A definition-site sweep over the cited source's
staged bytes finds no definition site for "defaultPrevented" — three of the four occurrence lines are
DOM-Standard cross-references and the fourth is the dfn panel of that external reference. Applying the
criterion before the r58 freeze would have rejected the citation ("S161 ... the cancelable/
defaultPrevented ground") and forced the corrected wording the r58 note eventually recorded after the
fact. Red-before for QUALITY.md criterion 111 is therefore established by command, not by assertion.

SCOPE NOTE (honest): the criterion does not say the term must be defined in the cited source; it says a
definition-site sweep must be RUN. The r58 claim passed freeze without one — the sweep here shows what
such a run would have found, and the finding is an absent definition site. The green side of the
criterion (a term that IS defined in its cited source) is probed separately in probes/d9-green-capslock.txt.
