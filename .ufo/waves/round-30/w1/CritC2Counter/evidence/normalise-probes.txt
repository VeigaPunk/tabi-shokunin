# normalise() probes (copy of the frozen function; the copy differs only by the removed trailing main() call)

unclosed <script> leaks JS text (closed one is stripped)
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><body><script>var q=\"secret policy words\";"
  out: "var q=\"secret policy words\";"
closed <script>
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><body><script>var q=\"secret policy words\";</script>"
  out: ""
HTML sniff is content-based: prose mentioning <html> is tag-stripped
  in : "RFC text mentions <html> and then <code>X</code> tag syntax."
  out: "RFC text mentions and then X tag syntax."
fragment page (no doctype/<html>) is not normalised at all
  in : "<p>A &amp; B</p>"
  out: "<p>A &amp; B</p>"
entities are decoded only in HTML mode
  in : "plain A &amp; B and &copy; here"
  out: "plain A &amp; B and &copy; here"
unknown entity stays literal in HTML mode
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><p>A &copy; B</p>"
  out: "A &copy; B"
<br> separates words (no word join)
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><p>word<br>next</p>"
  out: "word next"
zero-width space survives collapse and breaks a copy-pasted quote
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><p>consistent​over a sequence</p>"
  out: "consistent​over a sequence"
nbsp is collapsed to a space
  in : "<!DOCTYPE html><html><p>consistent&nbsp;over a sequence</p>"
  out: "consistent over a sequence"
