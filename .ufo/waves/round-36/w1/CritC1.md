  (digest in `frozenDigests`, `frozenBeforeData: true`) and the counter enforces the
  analogous digest for `scripts/check-adjacency.mjs` but not for its own round's frozen

  exit 0). But the counter's whole office is to not trust the tree, and wave-2's kill rule
  ("a drift row a wave-2 seat shows mis-audited is corrected … and re-measured") presumes
  the audited heads are the frozen ones. Judged on adjacency: the drift leg's completeness
  guarantee (307/307 at frozen heads, 3 moved rows named) is uncertified without the
  correction. Recommend the L1 fold the 4-line digest check (or a `measure.sh` provenance
  assertion) into the round-36 measurement before w2 closes C1; the frozen counter bytes
  then change, which is a counter-amendment decision for the canonical judge.
- Note the first-match note regex (line 438) as a documented fails-closed fragility: any
  earlier `:<digits>` token in a moved note fails the row even when a later token verifies.
  The frozen build tool's note shape (`verbatim at :N (also :N…)`) is immune.
