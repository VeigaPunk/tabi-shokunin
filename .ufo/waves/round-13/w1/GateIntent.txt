## Minimal policy recommendation

1. Keep HARD_BASELINE, PSTACK_PIN, the census file/rows, and every pre-existing historical blob unchanged. Do not solve this by moving pins, deleting census rows, or excluding SHONEN broadly; that would discard the evidence boundary rather than repair the policy.
2. Make the user gate's lesson-diff decision identity-aware: reject modifications/deletions to lesson paths that existed at the historical pin, while permitting only a newly added canonical round lesson path required by protocol. Do not interpret the filename pattern alone as proof that a new path is old history. Preserve other Pstack-path protections.
3. For SHONEN, allow only the one canonical regeneration, with the canonical seed/template and required marks checked; do not turn the exception into an unrestricted bypass. Apply that exact condition at each relevant baseline/protected-path and census comparison while retaining the old baseline and census values as historical evidence. A change to any other SHONEN content must still fail.
