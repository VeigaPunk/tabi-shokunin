There are two ways a client can establish this connection. In one method, when creating a classic WebDriver session, the WebDriver client sets the webSocketUrl capability to true to request BiDi to be enabled; the client then starts the browser with the WebSocket port open. In the other method, the WebDriver client starts the browser from the command line by passing the required flag and the desired port.
