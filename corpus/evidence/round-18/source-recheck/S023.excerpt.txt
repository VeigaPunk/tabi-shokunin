Note that many html elements like <button> have an implicitly defined role that is recognized by the role locator. Note that role locators do not replace accessibility audits and conformance tests, but rather give early feedback about the ARIA guidelines. When to use role locators We recommend prioritizing role locators to locate elements, as it is the closest way to how users and assistive technology perceive the page.
